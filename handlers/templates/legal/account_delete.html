Delete your account

You can close your Villum account yourself, without contacting anyone. The process is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to this instance{{if .Legal.BaseURL}} at {{.Legal.BaseURL}}{{end}}.
  2. Open Account settings.
  3. In the danger zone, type your username exactly as confirmation and submit the deletion form.

What is removed

  • Your username, display name, email address and password hash.
  • Every active session and API token, so no signed-in device keeps working.
  • Personal working data such as dice rolls, drafts, private notes, imports and notifications.
  • Links to external identities: Telegram, OIDC and calendar credentials, and push subscriptions.

What remains

Content that other people depend on — for example characters or campaigns shared with a group — is kept but no longer linked to a personal identifier; the author is shown as a deleted user. Residual copies may remain in backups for the operator's backup rotation period. Where law requires retention, that obligation takes precedence.

Cannot sign in?

If you have lost access to your account, {{if .Legal.ContactEmail}}contact {{.Legal.ContactEmail}}{{else}}contact the operator of this instance{{end}} and describe the account you want removed. The operator may ask you to prove that the account is yours.

For more detail on what is stored and why, read the privacy policy.
